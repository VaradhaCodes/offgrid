## Extra numbers worth citing

- **DVSE ablation @60 s velocity MAE**: base 2.35 → w/o augmentation 3.72 (+58 %), w/o MTN 3.65, w/o noise net 3.43, w/o loss matching 2.79 (+19 %).
- **DVSE backbone sweep @60 s**: GRU 2.35 / TCN 2.48 / LSTM 2.74 / Attention 2.74. Attention loses because the sequence is only 10 steps long.
- **CarSpeedNet @1 s**: a ResNet-style temporal model ties on RMSE (2.9) with MAE 1.4; WaveNet uses 239,937 params. The author states repeated-seed results are unavailable, so the ranking is not statistically supported.
- **MoE routing**: 3 of 8 experts are never activated; E5+E6 take ~85 % of activations; on unpaved road E6 rises 34.28 % → 47.04 %. K ablation: K=1 → IE 0.356, K=2 → 0.333, K=3 → 0.334, K=8 (dense) → 0.374 and ATE 10.99. N ablation: N∈{4,6,8,10,12} all give IE 0.329–0.335; **active experts saturate at 5**.
- **MoE v1 vs v2 numbers differ** (v1: IE 0.205, ATE 4.66, RTE 2.36 under a different protocol). Cite **v2** and say so.

## What changes our decision (max 10)

1. **1.28 s is too short; go to 2.56 s or longer.** CarSpeedNet is the only direct window sweep and it is monotone: 1 s → MAE 1.30 m/s, 2 s → 1.00, 4 s → 0.72. DVSE uses 10 s, the bicycle MoE uses 2 s. Our default should be **2.56 s with 5.12 s as a tested alternative**, not 1.28 s.
2. **100 Hz is more than enough; 200 Hz is unsupported by any of these.** CarSpeedNet deliberately downsamples 500 → **20 Hz**; DeepOdo and DVSE use **50 Hz**. Nobody shows a gain above 50 Hz. Recommend logging at 200–418 Hz but training at **50 Hz** (and testing 100 Hz), which cuts window tensors 2–4×.
3. **Gyro helps, but only when the frame is unresolved.** DeepOdo, DVSE and the bicycle MoE all feed accel+gyro; CarSpeedNet gets MAE 0.72 m/s from **accel only** at 4 s in a fixed mount. Since our phone is fixed to the down tube, run an accel-only vs accel+gyro ablation rather than assuming gyro is needed.
4. **Gaussian NLL is used exactly once — in the bicycle paper — and it is used *with* MSE, not instead of it.** MoE trains on MSE + NLL on a **diagonal** covariance, which is what the EKF then consumes as R. Copy this: diagonal covariance head, MSE + NLL, and feed the variance straight into the filter.
5. **Phone-frame input without alignment is proven to work — twice.** CarSpeedNet (raw 3-axis accel) and DeepOdo (raw accel/gyro/baro) both skip alignment. DVSE goes further and *learns* the phone→vehicle rotation. So we do **not** need a robust gravity-alignment front end for v1; it is an optional second stage.
6. **Rotation augmentation is the highest-value single trick, and it is cheap.** DVSE's ablation puts it above the noise net, the pose net and the loss matching: removing it costs +58 % velocity MAE. Implement it exactly as described — one random 3-axis Euler rotation applied identically to accel and gyro across a whole window. This partly substitutes for our tiny dataset.
7. **Sub-1 MB models are shown to work on ~10 h of data.** CarSpeedNet: **178 k params / 0.68 MiB** on 10.3 h train. DVSE's on-phone GRU is **572 KB RAM**. Do not start with the 7.18 M-param MoE; start at **~150–250 k params** for our 30 min and only scale if we log more.
8. **30 minutes is 20× below the smallest published corpus.** Smallest is CarSpeedNet's 10.3 h train; DeepOdo used 7.1 h; the bicycle MoE used >25 h. Plan for aggressive augmentation, heavy regularisation (DeepOdo used 30 % dropout on 7 h), and a **rider/route-held-out** split, not a random split — DVSE shows random splits overstate accuracy by 2–3× (1.2 vs 2.26 m/s; DeepTrack 1.12 vs 4.15).
9. **Handle stops outside the network.** DeepOdo gates the learned odometer off and switches to ZUPT+ZIHR using a moving-window std threshold. CarSpeedNet only claims stops qualitatively; DVSE and MoE say nothing. Build an explicit **stationarity detector + ZUPT** rather than trusting the regressor near 0 m/s.
10. **GNSS label latency is real and there is a cheap fix.** DVSE observes ~1 s GNSS lag behind the IMU at motion onset and takes **min over two alignments (0 and 1 sample of 1 Hz lag)** in the loss, worth 12–20 % error. We should either cross-correlate GNSS speed against accel magnitude per run to estimate the lag, or adopt DVSE's min-over-shifts loss.

### Gaps to flag in the master doc
- No paper states **normalisation**. We are choosing per-channel z-score from the training set with no evidential backing — mark as our own design decision.
- No paper releases **code**. Every architecture must be reimplemented; none of the four numbers can be reproduced directly.
- The bicycle MoE never states its **IMU sampling rate**, and v2 dropped the 2 s window statement that v1 contained — cite v1 for the window, v2 for the metrics.
- DeepOdo never states **N** (the GRU sequence length) or its parameter count.
